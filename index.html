HuskyFind is a mobile application designed to help University of Washington students, faculty, and staff reconnect with lost belongings. The platform allows users to search for lost items, post found items, and communicate through a secure messaging system. A key feature is the AI Privacy Scan, which automatically detects and blurs sensitive information on IDs and personal documents before they are posted publicly. The goal is to create a simple, safe, and accessible lost-and-found experience for the UW community.
